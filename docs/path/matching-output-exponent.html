Closing the exponent and logarithm for two output rules

Restricted-model result; not a general lower bound

The question

A partition can try to escape by making larger blocks. The hard family needs to cover both large and small blocks.

How it works

  1. The small-block branch forces many local probes whose list lengths multiply. Large catalogue blocks instead encounter independent matrix coefficients and many distinct residual polynomials.
  2. For the span option, many independent restrictions force a large set of evaluation coordinates; the charged full-grid option also rules out arbitrarily large blocks.
  3. An earlier construction paid logarithmic duplication in the graph size. Compact routing with three digit modes and linear-size superconcentrators removes that overhead. The final two allowed models have matching 2Θ(N2/3 log N) worst-case output cost.

What carries forward

The full routing and assembly argument is written. Lean checks finite cost components and the final inequality from its hypotheses; it does not check the unconditional lower-bound construction end to end.
